# Canada (seed)

Status: not started. No repo yet.

- Co-designed with a friend who is comfortable with git.

## Notes
- Premise idea: a relative has recently travelled to all 10 provinces and 3 territories, and made
  an adventure backpack from their travels.
- Goal: a fun way to learn cool, maybe obscure, things about Canada. Example: the world's largest
  axe in Nackawic, New Brunswick.
- Structure (decided 2026-09-30): 15 locks. One per province/territory (13), plus two final locks.
- Each stop uses a different puzzle type (decided 2026-09-30).
- Ending idea: the medallion, plus an invitation to ride the Toronto–Vancouver train
  (VIA Rail's *Canadian*) in a glass-dome car.

## Open questions
- 15 locks with 4 default lock types means each type repeats about 4 times. Buy extra lock
  types (directional, 5-letter, key...), or keep the defaults?
- What do 15 locks close (boxes, pouches, backpack pockets)? Weight and reset time.
- What do the two final locks use: answers from every stop, or their own puzzles?
- Which fact per province/territory? (candidates need checking against sources)
- What props carry the trip (postcards, passport stamps, photos, a map...)?
